Salvation

I just gave my life to Christ.

Heaven is rejoicing, and we are rejoicing with you. This page is for your first moments in Christ and your next steps as you begin a new life with Jesus.

Prayer of Salvation

Lord Jesus, I come to You today. I believe You died for me and rose again. I confess You as my Lord and Saviour. Forgive my sins, wash me clean, and fill me with Your Spirit. From today, I belong to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Next Steps

Your new walk with Christ deserves guidance, community, and growth.

Salvation is the beginning of a beautiful journey. As you start this new life in Christ, stay close to prayer, the Word, and a church family that can help you grow.

01

Keep Talking to God

Start praying daily. Speak to God honestly and expect Him to guide you.

02

Read Your Bible

Begin with the book of John and let scripture shape your new life with Christ.

03

Join Church Consistently

Worship with us, hear the Word, and grow in a community that strengthens your faith.

04

Tell Someone

Share your decision with someone you trust and let us celebrate and support you too.

Need Support?

We want to walk this journey with you.

If you just gave your life to Christ and want someone to stand with you in prayer or help you take the next step, you can contact us directly through our church email for now.
