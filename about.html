About Us

A church family carrying revival vision with warmth, excellence, and local impact.

Streams of Joy Johannesburg is an expression of Streams of Joy International, serving Johannesburg with vibrant worship, discipleship, prayer, and a Christ-centered culture of transformation.

Our Foundation Prayer. The Word. Community. Jesus.

We exist to reveal Christ, build believers, and create spaces where people can encounter God deeply.

Who We Are

Streams of Joy Johannesburg is a place to belong before you have everything figured out.

We are passionate about helping people discover Jesus, grow in faith, and walk in the purpose God has for their lives. We believe church should feel alive, welcoming, spiritually rich, and full of hope.

Inspired by the wider Streams of Joy family, this expression carries the same fire for prayer and transformation while serving Johannesburg with its own voice and grace.

Presence

We value authentic worship and spaces where people truly encounter God.

Prayer

People
