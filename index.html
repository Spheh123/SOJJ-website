Streams of Joy Johannesburg

Worship, community, and the presence of God.

We are building a Christ-centered community where people can encounter God, grow in the Word, and walk boldly in purpose. Whether you are new to church, returning to faith, or looking for a spiritual home, there is room for you here.

Our gatherings are marked by heartfelt worship, sound teaching, prophetic prayer, and a genuine atmosphere of love. We are inspired by the global vision of Streams of Joy International while serving Johannesburg with our own warmth, excellence, and local touch.
